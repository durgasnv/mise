# Product overview

Mise focuses on one household task: choose a practical dinner from ingredients the cook confirms are available, then help them cook it and record the outcome.

The core loop is ingredient confirmation, one recommendation with optional alternatives, review of quantity/label checks, cooking, explicit completion and optional feedback. Persistent pantry stock, weekly plans, editable recipes and source-backed nutrition support this loop.

A saved recipe or completed timer is not proof that dinner was cooked. Completion is explicit and user reported. Repeat cooking days, extra purchases, feedback, generation failures and operating costs can be measured with optional consent-based sharing; product claims need evidence from real households.

There are no implemented subscriptions, ingredient-count paywalls, automatic delivery, barcode scanning, IoT integrations or social feed. Pricing and external integrations remain hypotheses. The current software makes no guaranteed zero-waste, under-three-second, restaurant-quality or allergy-certification promise.
