# 1000 • Product Overview & Vision

## 1. Executive Summary

**Mise** (short for *Mise en place* — "everything in its place") is an artisanal AI culinary studio designed to transform whatever ingredients you have in your fridge and pantry into restaurant-quality feasts in seconds.

Inspired by the bold flavors, open-flame cooking, and effortless hospitality of **Loro Asian Smokehouse & Bar**, Mise replaces the stress of meal planning, recipe searching, and food waste with an interactive, sensory culinary workbench.

---

## 2. The Problem We Solve

Every day, home cooks and families face three major friction points:

1. **The "Pantry Paralysis" Dilemma**: Opening an eclectic fridge containing half an onion, leftover chicken, and a jar of chili crisp, but not knowing what to cook.
2. **Food Waste & Rising Costs**: Global households discard over 30% of edible groceries simply because recipes require specialized ingredients they don't have.
3. **Cluttered, Ad-Heavy Recipe Sites**: Traditional online food blogs force users to scroll through miles of ads, popups, and life stories before reaching the actual ingredients.

---

## 3. The Mise Solution & Core Philosophy

Mise approaches cooking like a professional restaurant pitmaster:

* **Ingredient-First (Zero Grocery Runs)**: You tell Mise what you have (`3 items`, `5 items`, `7 items`, or a freeform list), and Mise writes a gourmet recipe tailored specifically to your pantry.
* **Top 3 Distinct Variations**: Cooking is about preference. Mise automatically crafts **3 distinct culinary styles** (Fast Sauté, Comforting Braised Bowl, Crispy Cast-Iron Roast) in a stacked visual deck.
* **Interactive Stove Companion**: Cooking shouldn't end at reading text. Mise includes a **Hands-Free Cooking Mode** with giant text, automatic countdown timers, Web Audio dinner bell chimes, and browser voice reading.
* **Sensory Hospitality**: Every dish comes paired with a craft beverage pairing and a 2-ingredient companion side.

---

## 4. Target Audience

* **Home Cooks & Busy Professionals**: Looking to cook fast, healthy, and delicious weeknight dinners without recipe research.
* **Zero-Waste Advocates & Budget Cooks**: Wanting to utilize every scrap of produce, protein, and condiment in their kitchen.
* **Culinary Explorers & Foodies**: Inspired by smokehouse fusion, Asian aromatics, and creative flavor combinations.
* **College Students & Beginners**: Needing step-by-step guidance, ingredient swap suggestions, and visual timers.
