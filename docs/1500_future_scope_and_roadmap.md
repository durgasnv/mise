# 1500 • Future Scope & Roadmap

## 1. Vision & Evolution

Mise is designed to grow from an intelligent 3-to-7 ingredient generator into a **complete AI-driven home culinary operating system**.

---

## 2. Product Roadmap Phases

### Phase 1: Smart Barcode & Expiration Radar (Q3 2026)
* **Barcode & Receipt Scanning**: Users can scan grocery receipts to populate their digital fridge automatically.
* **Expiration Date Radar**: Prioritizes recipes that use produce, dairy, or proteins expiring within 48 hours to eliminate food waste.

---

### Phase 2: 1-Click Grocery & Pantry Delivery Integration (Q4 2026)
* **Instacart & Amazon Fresh API Integration**: If you are missing 1-2 key ingredients for a recipe, click *"Cart Missing Items"* to automatically populate an online grocery cart for 2-hour delivery.
* **Local Farmers Market & Butcher Radar**: Suggests fresh local smokehouse ingredients based on user location.

---

### Phase 3: Synchronized Multi-Dish Feast Planner (Q1 2027)
* **Multi-Dish Dinner Party Mode**: Coordinate a main, 2 sides, and a beverage with **synchronized multi-timers** so all dishes finish at the exact same moment.
* **Nutritional & Macro Tracking**: Automatic sync with Apple Health, MyFitnessPal, and Cronometer for high-protein, keto, and calorie-targeted diets.

---

### Phase 4: Smart Kitchen IoT & Voice Assistant Sidecar (Q2 2027)
* **Smart Oven & Cooker Integration**: Automatically push preheat temps and timers to connected appliances (*Anova Precision Oven, Traeger Smart Grills, Instant Pots*).
* **Alexa & Google Home Skills**: *"Alexa, tell Mise I have chicken, sweet corn, and butter"* — start cooking hands-free via kitchen smart displays.

---

### Phase 5: Community Smokehouse Social Feed (Q3 2027)
* **Community Cookbook Feed**: Share generated recipes, pitmaster twists, and photos.
* **Recipe Forking**: 1-click "fork" another user's recipe to swap ingredients for what is in your personal fridge.

---
